In a human body, health has less to do with strength than with circulation. When flow is interrupted, the tissue upstream swells and the tissue downstream starves. Most operational pain in a small company that I've experienced is really a circulation problem, not a strength problem. Information pools in one person. Decisions back up at the top. Approvals accumulate under the very honourable banner of quality control, until oversight has quietly become congestion. And the instinct, every time, is more control. More checking. But the fix is the opposite. Find the blockage. Open it. Let responsibility sit as close as it possibly can to the information. Work should flow through your company. It should not pool in you.

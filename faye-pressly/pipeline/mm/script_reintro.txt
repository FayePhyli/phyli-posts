I've read hundreds of business books over the last twenty years, and only one of them has changed how I see every company I've walked into since, and none of them spoke the language that I actually needed. For these two decades where I've run the inside of companies, it's meant I've read everything there was to read about how to grow an organisation, how to put a team together, how to shape a culture, how to run ops that could stand next to world-class businesses. And in all of these hundreds of books, only one of them spoke about a company the way I'd always experienced it. As a living thing with a body of its own. Something that has a shape, moves in a certain way, gets blocked, needs attention in some places and needs to be left alone in others. I was twenty-eight when I read it, and that's where this whole journey started.
The thing is, nobody inside the companies that I was running actually spoke that way. The day-to-day language inside an organisation was pretty stressful, and if you couldn't prove something with numbers or tangible data or facts, then as far as anyone else was concerned, it didn't exist, or it wasn't a priority. There was no space to sit back and ask what this organisation actually is. Who is she? How does she operate? How does she want to move? Where is she blocked? Where is she healing badly? Where does she need attending to? None of that language existed anywhere I worked, and it is the language that I speak. So I wrote the book, and it absolutely won't be for everyone. It is about growth, but it isn't about marketing and sales hacks. It's a different way of seeing your business, the inside of it, how it holds itself, how it moves, which is the way I've always seen businesses. I'm going to read it from here, and I'm going to share bits, one volume at a time.

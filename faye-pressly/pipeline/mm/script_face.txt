There was a Tuesday I've never forgotten. I'd spent the whole morning composing myself for a meeting. Difficult news, my own exhaustion, a private fear I'd told nobody. I ran the meeting well. I asked the right questions. I would have told you I'd given nothing away. That evening, one of my team members came to my desk and said, are you okay? We were all a bit worried about you in there. Every person in that room had spent an hour with a slightly raised heart rate, reading me, unable to give the work their whole attention. A nervous system can't be performed. State leaks through the face, the voice, the eyes, the pace. You can hold it for about an hour. But the team reads the rest.
